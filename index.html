01 // Operational Mandate

The Training and Development Unit (TDU) serves as the primary gateway and ongoing validation resource for all operating ranks within the community. Through simulation arrays, mechanical refinement loops, and comprehensive field logic evaluation, the TDU guarantees maximum operational coordination at all tiers of engagement.

02 // The Core Doctrine

🥇 Absolute Integrity

Grading criteria are binary, cold, and transparent. We do not issue passing logs based on reputation or tenure. Performance dictates survival.

🥈 Command Authority

Instructors maintain supreme command sovereignty within training blocks. Disruptive or non-compliant actions trigger immediate processing and dismissal.

🥉 Hyper-Adaptability

System mechanics shift and operational environments adapt. TDU operators ingest systems updates proactively, keeping our combat data fresh.
